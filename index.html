Bhashini connectionNot connected

Bhashini is the Government of India's National Language Translation Mission. It powers translation of your notes into 22 Indian languages and speech recognition for languages browsers can't handle, such as Bodo and Manipuri. Get a free User ID and ULCA API key by signing up at bhashini.gov.in/ulca, then open My Profile.

Your key stays in this browser. It is never put into the files you share with students.

AI assistantNot connected

AI cleans the transcript (misheard technical terms, punctuation, formulas, mixed-language speech), picks the important points, and writes topic-wise study notes with revision questions. Use Google Gemini with a free key from aistudio.google.com/apikey, or Anthropic Claude with a key from console.anthropic.com.

The key stays in this browser and never goes into files you share. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use the text sent to improve its products.
